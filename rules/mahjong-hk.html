Rules

Hong Kong Mahjong Rules

Traditional Hong Kong scoring and table flow only.

Back to Game Portal

Core setup

Four-player Hong Kong rules with flowers and seasons.

Tiles

144 tiles including flowers and seasons.

Dealing

East is dealer. Each player starts with 13 tiles; dealer starts with 14.

Goal

Complete 4 melds plus a pair to win.

Winning and scoring

Minimum 1 fan required under Hong Kong scoring.

Minimum fan

You must have at least 1 fan to win.

Fan system

Scoring follows the Hong Kong fan table.

Dealer continuation

Dealer keeps the seat after a win.

Turn flow

Draw, declare, discard with standard priority.

Priority

Win, then kong or pong, then chow.

Draw and discard

Draw a tile, declare a meld if applicable, then discard.

Rounds

Rounds rotate by wind with East as the initial dealer.
