Hong Kong Big Two Rules

Classic Hong Kong ranking and play order only.

Back to Game Portal

Card order

Hong Kong ranking with suit priority.

Rank

3 is lowest, 2 is highest.

Suit order

Diamonds, clubs, hearts, spades. The 2 of spades is highest overall.

Opening lead

Game starts with the 3 of diamonds.

Valid hands

Standard Hong Kong five-card ranking.

Singles and pairs

Play singles, pairs, or triples.

Five-card hands

Straight, flush, full house, four of a kind, straight flush.

Comparison

Beat the current hand with the same number of cards.

Play flow

Passes reset the trick.

Passing

After three passes, the trick resets.

Turn order

Players continue clockwise from the current leader.

Winning

First player to shed all cards wins the round.
